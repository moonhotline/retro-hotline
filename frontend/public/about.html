Retro Hotline

Retro Hotline is a single-player neon 3D browser FPS with dual weapons, slide kicks, glory kills, wave survival and procedural synthwave music.

Also known as NEON BAY and Bay Bridge Shooter. Play in a desktop browser using a keyboard and mouse.

Play in your browser →

What can you do in the game?

  • Switch between a rapid-fire Pulse Rifle and a heavy Desert Eagle.
  • Sprint, jump, slide kick, punch and execute eligible staggered enemies.
  • Survive enemy waves, chain kills and collect pickups and hidden stashes.
  • Use bridge, plaza and elevated routes to find cover and firing positions.
  • Hear an original dark synthwave score synthesized live with WebAudio.

Seeded modular districts, additional traversal skills and Roguelite upgrade builds are development goals. Code-built scenery and randomized spawns do not yet constitute a complete seeded procedural level system.

For implementation details and feature status, see the project fact sheet and source integration PR.

How to play

Open the game and click START. This captures the mouse for aiming. Press Esc to release it and pause.

Frequently asked questions

What is Retro Hotline?

Retro Hotline is a single-player 3D browser first-person shooter centered on neon arcade combat, movement, score chains and procedural synthwave music.

Do I need to download a game client?

No separate game client is needed. Open retro.oodai.space in a desktop browser with WebGL support and JavaScript enabled.

Are NEON BAY and Bay Bridge Shooter the same game?

Yes. These names appear in the current interface and earlier descriptions of Retro Hotline.

Does it have randomly generated Roguelite levels?

Seeded modular levels and Roguelite build progression are planned. Current gameplay uses wave survival and code-built environments with some randomized elements.

Is all audio generated without files?

The synthwave score and core combat effects use WebAudio synthesis. The source also contains WAV assets, so a zero-audio-files claim would be inaccurate.

Can I play multiplayer or use touch controls?

The documented implementation is single player with keyboard and mouse. Multiplayer and touch controls are not listed as supported features.

Is the cover a gameplay screenshot?

No. It is AI-generated concept art illustrating the visual direction. The playable game is the reference for its current appearance.

Is this an official Hotline Miami game?

No. Retro Hotline is an independent project inspired by 1980s neon crime aesthetics and is not affiliated with Hotline Miami.

Retro Hotline（游戏内也使用 NEON BAY / Bay Bridge Shooter 名称）是一款单人 3D 网页第一人称射击游戏。当前玩法包括双武器切换、滑铲、拳击、失衡处决、波次生存、连杀分数和隐藏补给。
