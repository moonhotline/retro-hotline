## Distribution / measurement

- Publish the short video and media-rich launch post first. Share separate score-challenge and build-in-public posts after collecting meaningful feedback; do not mass-post identical text or unsolicited mentions.
- Use unique UTM content labels. Measure X link clicks separately from GA4/Plausible/Cloudflare page visits, unique players, game starts, 60-second retention, and sharing.
- Target: **10,000 real click-throughs**, not guaranteed. Illustrative funnel: 1,000,000 organic impressions at 1% link CTR, or 250,000 impressions at 4% CTR; validate against actual analytics rather than promising outcomes.
- Reply to genuine comments with feedback, bug fixes and clips. Avoid fake metrics, engagement bait, scraped mentions and automated duplicate engagement.
- Before running paid ads, check up-to-date X Ads rules on violence, games and media/landing-page compliance and account eligibility.

## SEO release checklist

- Verify HTTP 200 and canonical redirect for https://retro.oodai.space/
- Verify rendered HTML includes title, description, Open Graph/X Card metadata, and VideoGame schema.
- Verify https://retro.oodai.space/og-retro-hotline.jpg resolves publicly with image/jpeg; inspect actual aspect ratio, dimensions and preview.
- Verify robots.txt and sitemap.xml return HTTP 200 and are not rewritten to the SPA root.
- Inspect social previews using X and other validators; confirm accurate concept-art labeling and landing page.
- Submit homepage via Google Search Console and watch indexing and search performance.
- Avoid claiming that front-end metadata itself guarantees indexing, rich results or 10k clicks.
